# Appendix H. Demo Data Specification

> Part of `docs/brief/02-appendices/`. Normative. Read with the master brief and the reference architecture.

A deterministic seed that makes the product convincing in a demo and doubles as test data.

- The platform brand in the demo is Nibras; each demo tenant has its own school name, logo, and colors to demonstrate white-labeling
- **Two tenants:** one single-campus school of about 600 students, and one group with two campuses, to prove isolation and group reporting
- Nursery to grade 12, about 25 students per section, realistic Arabic and English names, guardians with siblings across grades, split-custody and sponsored-payer examples
- About 60 staff with departments, loads, and a few part-time teachers; a published timetable generated by the solver
- A full previous academic year (results, report cards, promotions) and a current year at mid-term with attendance, coursework, marks in progress, behavior records, clinic visits, and several at-risk students with clear explanations
- Fee plans with paid, partial, overdue, and refunded examples; one cashier day to close
- An admissions pipeline with records in every stage, and a re-enrollment campaign in progress
- Requests of every major type in every state, with approvals waiting for the principal
- Announcements, conversations, a booked conference day, a survey with responses
- One user per role with simple demo credentials shown on a demo-only login helper. **Demo credentials and the login helper must never exist in a production build.**
- One-click reset to the seeded state, and a guided tour script per role that follows the golden-path demo
