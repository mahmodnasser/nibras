# 01. Questions and Assumptions

> Group A. Every open point in `docs/project/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` restated with the default now in force, what it costs to be wrong, which plan documents and phases lean on it, and what evidence would change it. Nothing here blocks the plan: a default is a decision until it is overturned.

**How to read this document.** `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` is the register; this document is the impact analysis behind it. The questions are grouped exactly as the decisions workshop in `docs/plan/REVIEW_GUIDE.md` takes them, so the product owner can walk the five blocks in ninety minutes with this document open. Thirty questions are open, the count `REVIEW_GUIDE.md` gives; the latest, question 31 (the daily-sheet amendment of Appendices B and C), was entered on 2026-09-26 from the Academics sheet's open point 1. Question 30 (the billing count) was decided by the product owner on 2026-09-26 with the recommended answer and ADR-0027 (Accepted), and moved to section 6.3. Twenty-three are on the workshop agenda, including question 29 (the Wellbeing check-in on the device, the highest-scoring open question) in the scope block, where the privacy officer takes it with the product owner. Seven (17, 19, 21, 22, 27, 28, 31) are not on the agenda because they can be answered in writing; they sit in the block their answer belongs to, and the product owner answers them in `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` before writing the Group A approval note in `PROJECT_STATE.md`. Three of the seven have a harder deadline: question 27 before the first Phase 2 attendance slice (RISK-41), question 28 before the MVP cut line is confirmed (RISK-42), and question 31 before Phase 2 starts. Questions 27 and 28 reached `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` with ADR-0019, which settled four conflicting values (section 6.2) and deliberately left these two open; questions 29, 30 (now decided) and 31 came from the Group C service sheets. Phases are the seven in master brief Section 28. Plan documents are named by their number in `docs/plan/PLAN_SPEC.md`.

**Status legend.** *Default in force* means the plan is written as though the default were the answer. *Proposed* means a decision record in `docs/project/DECISIONS/` states the answer but still reads "Proposed, awaiting product owner confirmation"; the plan builds on it as a default in force, never as a settled fact, until the product owner accepts or overturns the record. *Settled* means a record the product owner has **Accepted** closed it; today that is ADR-0019 and ADR-0027. Owner is the person whose silence accepts the default. The "Evidence that would change it" cell ends with the mitigation that limits the cost if the default is wrong. **L** is the likelihood that the default is wrong and **I** the impact if it is, on the 1 to 5 scales of `18-risk-register.md` Section 1; **Score** is L x I; **In the register** names the RISK identifiers in document 18 that cover the question, or `none`. A question that scores 12 or more always names one. Kit-lint rules R24 and R33 check the columns (ADR-0022), and this document is the list of open questions, so it has no separate Open points section.
