# 01. Questions and Assumptions

> Group A. Every open point in `docs/project/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` restated with the default now in force, what it costs to be wrong, which plan documents and phases lean on it, and what evidence would change it. Nothing here blocks the plan: a default is a decision until it is overturned.

**How to read this document.** `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` is the register; this document is the impact analysis behind it. The questions are grouped exactly as the decisions workshop in `docs/plan/REVIEW_GUIDE.md` takes them, so the product owner can walk the five blocks in ninety minutes with this document open. Six questions (17, 19, 21, 22, 27, 28) are not on the workshop agenda because they can be answered asynchronously; they sit in the block their answer belongs to. Questions 27 and 28 reached `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` with ADR-0019, which settled four conflicting values and deliberately left these two open, so the agenda table in `REVIEW_GUIDE.md` still counts twenty-six and needs the same two rows. Phases are the seven in master brief Section 28. Plan documents are named by their number in `docs/plan/PLAN_SPEC.md`.

**Status legend.** *Default in force* means the plan is written as though the default were the answer. *Settled* means a decision record closed it. Owner is the person whose silence accepts the default.

## 8. Answers to master brief Section 27

Section 27 lists thirteen decisions to confirm before starting. This is the state of each.

1. **.NET 10 or .NET 8.** Default in force: .NET 10 LTS. ADR-0001, Proposed. Open question 1. The fallback list in Section 19 stays in the brief until the product owner confirms, so a late reversal has a written path.
2. **SaaS multi-tenant, on-premises, or both.** Default in force: both, from one image set. Open question 2. Every deployment mode is in `15-deployment-and-operations.md`; the appliance for Windows hosts is ADR-0016.
3. **Languages beyond English and Arabic.** Default in force: no; every string externalised so a third is configuration. Open question 11.
4. **Target countries.** Default in force: Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates, Jordan. Open question 3. The three time zones in the culture test, the compliance rows in Section 33 and the regional plug-in order all follow from it.
5. **Which Tier 2 modules matter most.** Default in force: none until Tier 1 is complete; Phase 5 in the order Section 28 lists. Open question 4.
6. **Local AI hardware, or AI ships later.** Default in force: rungs 1 and 2 ship with the product, rung 3 later and off by default. Open question 5. ADR-0015 makes the product complete at rung 1, so only the Ai service's phase depends on the answer.
7. **Wolverine or a thin in-house layer.** Decided by the architect: Wolverine behind the messaging building block. ADR-0004, Proposed. Open question 6 stays open only for the product owner to be informed.
