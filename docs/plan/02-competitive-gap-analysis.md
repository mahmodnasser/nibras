# 02. Competitive Gap Analysis

**Purpose.** Master brief Section 26 requires a competitive gap analysis in Phase 0, and Appendix P supplies its seed: a field table, a list of claimed differences, a list of competitor strengths, and a matrix of signature features. Appendix P states that every one of its claims is a hypothesis until it is checked against the vendor's current public documentation. This document is that check. It records what each product is, where it is strong, where a school outgrows it, which of ten named capabilities it has, and what that means for the Nibras roadmap.

**Method.** Every product in Appendix P section P.1 was checked on **2026-09-20** with forty web calls in total: ten searches to locate vendor pages, twenty-eight fetches of vendor feature pages, developer portals, help centres, pricing pages and public repositories, and two further searches where a vendor page could not be fetched. Vendor sources were preferred; where only a third-party source or a search snippet was available, the footnote says so. Each cell in the capability matrix carries a footnote number that resolves to a source URL and the date checked.

**Second check, 2026-09-26.** A verification pass for the cells left unverified, aimed first at the capabilities that carry the Nibras column and the signature features document 32 lists as unverified in all ten, made **forty-six web calls**: thirty-nine searches, restricted to the vendors' own domains wherever the search allowed it, and seven fetches. **All seven fetches were refused by the network egress policy of the session that ran the check** (api-docs.veracross.com, help.classter.com, developer.blackbaud.com, www.classter.com, www.powerschool.com, www.toddleapp.com, gibbonedu.org); the refusal came from the checking environment, not from the vendors. Every finding of the second check therefore rests on the search snippet of a vendor page (type **S**, sources 32 to 54) and on nothing else; a finding the snippet did not state plainly was left unverified. The second check moved **12 matrix cells** out of unverified (2 to yes, 10 to partial) and found a comparable form for ten of the signature features document 32 had as unverified in all ten ("Second check of the signature features" below). The same rules apply: silence stays unverified, and "no" needs the vendor's word or the product's shape.

**Unverified cells.** A cell reads **unverified** when no vendor page checked on either check date (2026-09-20 or 2026-09-26) states the capability either way. Absence of a feature from a marketing page is not proof of absence, so silence is recorded as unverified rather than as "no". A cell reads **no** only when the vendor's own page says so, or when the product's published shape rules it out (for example, a commercial subscription product with no published licence is "no" for open source). This is deliberately conservative: the competitive claims that survive it can be spoken to a buyer.

**Ministry-integrated systems** in Appendix P section P.1 are mandated per country, are not products a school chooses, and have no vendor documentation to check. They are kept in the field table with their row marked as such and are excluded from the capability matrix.
