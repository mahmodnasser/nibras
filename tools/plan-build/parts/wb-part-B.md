### Coverage notes for part B

Every requirement in this part's scope is built by at least one slice above; none is left to a gate alone. Three points a reader should know:

- REQ-ACA-027 and REQ-ACA-028 are Tier 2 and are built by SL-ACA-218 and SL-ACA-209, but both slices wait on the Appendix B amendment the Academics sheet records as open point 1 (and, for online classes, a participation command to Attendance, open point 4). If the amendment has not landed by phase 2, the two slices move to phase 5 with the other Tier 2 work rather than shipping without a permission.
- REQ-MOB-002 names nurse mode alongside the gate, bus attendant and kiosk modes. SL-MOB-200 and SL-MOB-201 build the shared mode shell and three of the four modes; nurse mode's screens arrive with Wellbeing in phase 5 on that shell, and REQ-MOB-008 (SL-MOB-203) keeps clinic data off the device either way.
- The MOB requirements are cross-cutting: once SL-MOB-202 to SL-MOB-213 land, they join the definition of done for every later mobile slice, enforced by the golden and offline sync tests named in document 17 Section 3.
