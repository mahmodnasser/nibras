### Coverage notes for part D

Every phase 5 requirement in this part's scope is built by at least one slice above, except the two Tier 3 extension points listed first. Points a reader should know:

- REQ-OPS-014 (cafeteria wallet) and REQ-OPS-015 (boarding and hostel) are Tier 3 and are designed in the Operations sheet as reserved `cafeteria` and `boarding` schemas that are deliberately not created in v1 (Operations sheet, Decisions in force: creating empty schemas early commits a design nobody has reviewed).
